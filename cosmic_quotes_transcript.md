**Celestial Oracle:** In the velvet darkness of infinity... there exists an island of light. A spiral of four hundred billion suns, swirling in an eternal dance across a canvas one hundred thousand light-years wide. We call it... the Milky Way. But what we see is but a whisper of what truly exists. Ninety percent of our galaxy's mass dwells in shadow... invisible, untouchable. Dark matter—an ocean of mystery that cradles our island of light. A cosmic web woven from threads we cannot see, holding the visible universe in its silent embrace. At the heart of our galaxy... dwells a guardian of incomprehensible power. Sagittarius A-star—a supermassive black hole, four million times the mass of our Sun. An ancient sentinel, invisible yet omnipresent... bending light itself, holding the cosmic dance together with gravity's eternal embrace. Like rivers of light flowing through the cosmos... spiral arms sweep across our galaxy. The Perseus Arm, the Sagittarius Arm, the Scutum-Centaurus... cosmic currents where gas collapses into fire, where stars are born from darkness, where creation never ceases. And where do we dwell in this vast cosmic spiral? Twenty-six thousand light-years from the center... in a small spur called the Orion Arm. If this galaxy were the size of a human body... our entire solar system would be no larger than a single atom. Yet within this atom... consciousness awakens. The universe opens its eyes and gazes upon itself. And in four billion years... our galaxy shall merge with another. Andromeda, our sister spiral, draws ever closer. Two galaxies becoming one in a cosmic dance of transformation. We spin through space at half a million miles per hour... completing one orbit around our galaxy's heart every two hundred million years. One cosmic year. One breath in the life of the galaxy. Our entire human story—from cave paintings to starships—is but a heartbeat... a single moment... in the eternal waltz of stars.
